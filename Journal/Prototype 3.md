**Journal Entry 1**
I got really inspired by the environment/atmosphere in the book (and movie) "Annihilation" by Jeff Vandermeer. Specifically the Shimmer, which is a giant, half-transparent wall which can only be traversed through a small passage way. Although that was my original inspiration, my prototype now is very different from it. However, I did stick with the idea of having some sort of tunnel/passage for the player to go through in order to get to the main area. 

My scene is separated into 3 places: the first room (right now just a black box, which might change if I can find something that might make more sense within the context of the space), the tunnel and the actual environment with the purple stone and arches. I kept the original wind sound from the template, but added extra atmospheric sounds in the tunnel and main area to go along with it. In the main area, the arches also make a sound as the player goes through them. I hope to add more interactive elements like that throughout.

I want the atmosphere of this environment to feel both beautiful and eerie, overall surreal. I also want a big emphasis on sound, which I think was successful in the tunnel specifically. I woulkd like to add that effect to the rest of the environment. More sound and more ambiance! I also need to work out the transitions (from and to the tunnel) for the sounds. What stays? What stops playing immediately?

**Journal Entry 2**
Worked more on environment. I thought the open space felt too empty and although I had height with the tall surrounding cliffs, I wanted that height to be reachable by the player (to add more exploration/more areas of interest). In that regard, I added a tower towards the middle. This makes the space seem more interactive since the moment the player arrives through the tunnel, they are met with the arches to the left and the tower (and its stairs) on the right. It is hopefully obvious (by the bright blue gems surrounding them) that these areas might impact the gaming experience in some way. Now that I have these areas marked, I need them to actually *do* something when interacted with. Do they mean something? Is it a sort of puzzle? Or message?
