**Journal Entry 1**
My original idea for the two enhancements were:
1. Interacting with certain areas of the environment unlocks new/secret locations.
2. Collecting objects and dropping them in the pit affects the environment. Maybe there is some sort of implied creature down there (moaning/growling sounds) which can be quietened this way.

I implemented the core mechanics of these two ideas, and now the gameplay is much more exciting. Even if there is no real plot and this is still essentially just a walking simulator, the atmosphere feel more alive. Like this could actually be some other world where things happen and decisions matter.

What I'm trying to balance now is giving the player free range to explore while also subconsciously guiding them towards certain areas (like the arches and the tower, which the player must explore before interacting with the pit). The blue gems are there to alert the player to exactly that, acting as a sort of linear guide. The fact that the environment is so monochrome also works very well for this since it makes the blue stand out even more.

I now need to figure out *how* to better make the player understand the significance of the pit, and the orbs in accordance with the pit. I'll try to add text, maybe in the for of UI instructions or maybe something more plot/environment-related, that could lead the player to doing what the game expects of them. Do I add story elements? Mention the creature in the pit? Or allow the player to infer from the sound and level design? I also need the orb dropping mechanic to actually mean/do something.
